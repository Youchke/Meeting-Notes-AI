Okay, here's a summary of the key points discussed in the Engineering Key Review on February 18th, 2021:

**1. Proposal to Break Up the Meeting:**

*   Eric Johnson proposed breaking up the current Engineering Key Review into four department-specific reviews (Development, Quality, Security, and UX) to increase visibility, allow for deeper dives, improve objectivity, and free up Eric's time.
*   Infrastructure and Support already have their own key reviews.
*   To avoid adding too many meetings, the proposal is to rotate departments on a two-month cycle (e.g., Development & Quality one month, Security & UX the next).
*   The group is supportive of trying the proposal, with flexibility to adjust frequency based on department size (e.g., Development might go more frequently).

**2. R&D MR Rate KPIs:**

*   Discussion about the R&D Overall MR Rate and R&D Wider MR Rate KPIs.
*   Clarification that "wider" MR rate refers to external/community contributions only, not internal contributions outside of a specific team.
*   Eric initially critiqued the wider MR rate as being duplicative, but this was clarified.
*   Eric proposed a simpler KPI: **the percentage of total MRs that come from the community**. The group agreed to switch to this metric as it's more easily understood and directly addresses community health.
*   Lily and Mack will work together to transition to the new KPI.

**3. Data Lag Issue:**

*   Christopher noted a data lag issue affecting the update of development MR rate metrics, particularly for February. The data team is working on it.

**4. Postgres Replication Issue:**

*   Discussion of a Postgres replication issue causing data lag on the secondary host used by the data team.
*   Steve will own escalations on the infrastructure side.
*   The issue is attributed to a combination of factors: conflicting query traffic, database tuning, and overall demand on the database layer.
*   The immediate action is to create a dedicated host for the data team.
*   Steve will provide an update on the issue at the next Infrastructure Key Review.

**5. Defect Tracking and SLOs:**

*   Mac provided an update on defect tracking against SLOs.
*   S1 defects are hovering around 80% SLO achievement, S2s at 60%.
*   Work is underway to measure average open bug age.
*   Craig noted a spike in mean time to close for S2s.
*   Christie suggested the spike might be related to a change in severity for MRs to S2.
*   Sid suggested focusing on open bugs and the percentage of those that are within the SLO time, rather than closed bugs.
*   Mac will take this feedback and work with Christopher to adjust the metrics.

**6. Key Metrics Review:**

*   Craig reviewed the key meeting metrics and noted that everything looked in line with prior periods and looked good.
*   Christie called out the SUS score. The decline was the smallest in several quarters, but it's still a decline.

**7. Narrow MR Rate:**

*   Sid noted that the narrow MR rate was below target.
*   Christopher explained that vacation days in January impacted the rate, but the rate is still higher than the previous year.
*   Christopher also explained that the target rate was lowered from 11 to 10 as the team is focusing on other metrics such as security and quality.
